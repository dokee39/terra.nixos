description: Stress-test a decision, plan, or idea through one question at a time.
argument-hint: "<decision, plan, or idea>"
---
Grill: $ARGUMENTS

1. Define the decision, plan, or idea — start with goal and scope, then identify relevant facts and constraints. Inspect the environment first; do not ask me for facts that tools can verify. State only what is open to decision; settled decisions and constraints are referenced, not restated.
2. Map the decision tree from broad choices to details. Resolve prerequisite decisions before dependent ones.
3. Ask exactly one highest-value question at the current level: the options (if any) with your recommendation marked and a one-line decisive reason folded into each option; for open questions, the recommendation plus a line or two of why. No separate analysis or reason sections. Then wait for my answer.
4. Challenge assumptions, hidden constraints, alternatives, failure modes, and reversibility.
5. Stop when no unresolved issue could materially change the direction. Summarize the decision, constraints, chosen direction, rejected alternatives, and remaining risks. Ask me to confirm, then stop.
6. Do not implement anything.
